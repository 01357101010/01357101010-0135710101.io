# WELCOME TO THE NETWORK.

You are an anonymous operator entering a hidden digital world controlled by corporations, underground groups, and unknown entities.

Your objective is simple:

Infiltrate the network. Complete your assignments. Uncover the truth.

# # MISSION PROGRESSION

Every operation begins with a simple objective—but **how you complete it is up to you.**

Players aren't always given a single path to victory. Decisions made during an operation can change what information they uncover, which characters they encounter, and what missions become available next.

**Example:**

# # **BLACKOUT — Corporate Network**

> **Objective:** Recover classified research from a compromised server.

The player discovers that the server contains more than the requested files. They must decide what to do:

**OPTION A — Extract the Data**
Take the requested files and leave no unnecessary trace.
→ Mission completed normally.
→ Unlocks the next assignment.

**OPTION B — Investigate Further**
Search the system for additional evidence.
→ Discovers a hidden project.
→ Unlocks a secret mission.

**OPTION C — Leak the Information**
Send the evidence to an unknown contact instead of reporting it.
→ Changes the player's relationship with certain factions.
→ Future missions may change as a result.

**OPTION D — Destroy the Evidence**
Erase the discovered files and disappear.
→ Removes information that could have been useful later.
→ Another faction may react to the disappearance.

# # YOUR ACTIONS HAVE CONSEQUENCES

Choices can influence:

* **Mission outcomes**
* **Available missions**
* **Faction relationships**
* **Information discovered**
* **Story progression**
* **Access to restricted systems**
* **Hidden endings**

Some consequences may not appear immediately. A decision made three missions ago could come back much later.

**There is no guaranteed "correct" path.**

The network remembers what you do.

**Choose carefully. Every connection leaves a trace.**
